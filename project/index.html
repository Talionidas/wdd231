My Running Journey

Running has become one of my favorite hobbies over the past few years. After returning home from my mission, it became a great way to stay physically active while also helping me clear my mind. What started as short runs gradually turned into a personal goal of completing a marathon.

Earlier this year I finished my first marathon at the Zürich Marathon. Since then I've been looking for new races and challenges throughout Switzerland. Building this website combines two of my interests: web development and running.

My hope is that this site can help other runners discover events they may not have known about and encourage them to set their own running goals.
